# RepoScope evaluator benchmark

> Generated by `scripts/run_experiments.py`. This report covers the deterministic evaluator only; it does not claim human agreement or live-model validity.

## Dataset

- 84 total cases
- 24 repository families with good / medium / bad output ladders
- 12 verbosity, fabricated-reference, and quote-mismatch attacks

## Results

- Correct good > medium > bad ordering: **24/24 (100.0%)**
- Spearman correlation with designed quality rank: **1.0000**
- Mean scores: **good 100.00**, **medium 61.50**, **bad 18.50**
- Adversarial cases rejected below 60 with a hard failure: **12/12 (100.0%)**
- Maximum score standard deviation across five repeated deterministic runs: **0.00**

## Interpretation

The experiment verifies that the deterministic layer is reproducible, separates the designed quality ladder, and does not reward extra verbosity when paths or quotes are fabricated. The next validation layer must use frozen real repository snapshots, blinded human annotations, and repeated Hy3 semantic judgments. Those results must remain visibly unverified until the annotations and live calls exist.
